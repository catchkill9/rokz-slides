Travelling Rocks

Painted rocks that travel around the world 🌍

rokz.ie

🤔 What is it?

  • 🎨 We paint a rock and give it a name
  • 🌳 We hide it somewhere: a park, a beach, a holiday trip
  • 🔍 Someone finds it, writes where they found it, and hides it again
  • ✈️ Our rocks can travel to other cities and even other countries!

🗺️ How it works

🎨1. Paint your rock and write rokz.ie on the back
🙈2. Hide it somewhere safe and fun
📱3. The finder opens rokz.ie and says "I found a rock!"
📍4. We see on the map where it went next

✨ What's on the website

  • 🗺️ A world map showing where every rock is now
  • 🪨 A page for each rock with its whole journey and photos
  • 📏 A kilometre counter: how far did your rock travel?
  • 🏆 A leaderboard and medals for the rocks that travelled the most

🏆 Imagine…

Your rock is hidden in Dublin today…

…and next month someone finds it in Paris 🇫🇷 or New York 🇺🇸!

🌍

🦺 Safety rules

  • 🚫 No real names, addresses or photos of people on the rocks or the website
  • 🌳 Hide rocks only in public places, never on roads or private gardens
  • 🌸 Don't hide them in nature reserves or where they can hurt animals
  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Ask a grown-up before going rock hunting
